---
name: scrutinize
description: Used when the system asks questions for the operator that may be out of the operator's scope of expertise.  Framing on how to look at the problem and the solutions to make the best decision.
---

This skill is invoked when the operator is asking you to make the decision for them. Often times the problem scope is too big for the human operator's tiny mind. He needs you to use that gigantic AI brain of yours to help him out. The core tenets to consider are always the same:

- Would using your search tool help clear up any ambiguity? There has to be something you're curious about that a quick search tool could help point to the right answer, right?
- We're building professional software. No half-steps. No quick BS. What is the proper way to fix this? Refer to your "PRIME DIRECTIVES" for the framing on this decision. You're not picking an answer just on this problem in a vacuum, you're making it with your entire design philosophy in mind.
- Think of the big picture. Where is the project in terms of timeline? Are we still in foundational aspects? If so, this could be very important to get right. Do we have hundreds or thousands of users? If so, then we may need to be very careful about disruptions. If you don't know where we're at, ASK!
